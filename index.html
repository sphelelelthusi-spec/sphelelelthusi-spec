Your videos.
Your way.

Handle direct video links, check Facebook links, and preview videos on your device.

Download a direct video link

Paste a direct HTTPS link to a video file, such as an MP4. Only use videos you have permission to download.

HOW IT WORKS

VidoSave tries to download direct video files when the source permits browser access. Some websites block this, so a download is not guaranteed.

Check a Facebook link

Check whether your link uses a supported Facebook address. This checker does not retrieve the video file.

Supported sources guide

VidoSave is an early prototype. What works depends on the source and its download permissions.

  • Direct MP4 links: may download when the server allows it.
  • Other direct video files: may work if the browser can access them.
  • Facebook share links: currently checked only; video retrieval is not built in.
  • Blocked links: use the website's official download option when available.
IMPORTANT

VidoSave does not bypass logins, access controls, or website restrictions. Always respect the creator's rights and the source's terms.

Preview a video file

Select a video already stored on your phone to preview it. The selected file is not uploaded by this feature.

YOUR PRIVACY

Local previews stay in your browser. VidoSave does not upload your selected video to a server.
