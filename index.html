Handle direct video links and preview video files on your device.

Download a direct video link

Paste a direct HTTPS link to a video file, such as an MP4. Only use files you have permission to download.

HOW IT WORKS

VidoSave will try to download a video when the source allows it. If the source blocks browser access, the link will open directly instead. This does not guarantee a download.

Check a Facebook link

Check whether your link uses a supported Facebook address.

Preview a video file

Select a video already stored on your phone to preview it.

YOUR PRIVACY

Local previews stay in your browser. VidoSave does not upload your selected video to a server.
